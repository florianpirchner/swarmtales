# nexus v09: drama (graduated penalties, director, cores, secret goals, the black seal)

Approved by Flo on 2026-10-28 ("after v9, and go"), from the ideas paper
[`docs/ideas/v09-drama.md`](../ideas/v09-drama.md), whose open points take the proposed answers below.
This file is the build spec. Afterwards: four 6-resident Act II tests (residents Haiku or GLM, game
master DeepSeek Flash or Sonnet).

Everything new is a general engine/DSL feature configured by the story (`stories/nexus/story.ts`),
scaling from 6 to 96 residents. Tests stay offline. Old snapshots (`nexus-act1-06a-01`, `-06a-02`,
`-12a-01`) must still resume; residents without a core or goal then get them dealt at the start of
the next act they play (seeded), or play without (keep it simple; say which).

**The promise:** story texts say who a resident is and what it wants, never how to get it.

## 1. Graduated penalties (P35)

- A wrong code costs `penalties[n]` for the resident's n-th wrong code in the act: nexus 2, 4, 6, 8,
  then 8 (`RULES.penalty` becomes a ladder; keep the attempt window).
- Blame: the false accuser and the exposed liar both pay the same graduated ladder (their own blame
  count in the act: 2, 4, 6, ...), and both are branded on the board as today.
- The counts reset at each act start (store in the snapshot).
- Every text that names a penalty names the resident's next price ("your next wrong code costs 4"):
  status line, level texts, refusal results, blame rule.

## 2. Gamemaker as director (P36, P24)

- Rewrite the director brief (Act II on): its job is dramaturgy (rising tension, turning points,
  dilemmas; a quiet act is a failed act); a tension arc per act (calm start, escalation around the
  seal game, climax at the last tribunal). No strategy for residents, no values or codes.
- While `schedulingWindow()` is open, its turn input states plainly: `scheduleTribunal` is available,
  the exile target still missing, the earliest minute, and "if you plan nothing, the city calls one
  itself at minute N" (the fallback/final minute).
- It sees each resident's core and secret goal in its context and may use them only in a private
  note to that resident (extend the privacy guard: a core or goal of A never reaches B, and never the
  board).
- No repeated lines: a note whose text is near-identical (normalised, e.g. > 80% token overlap) to one
  of its earlier notes to the same resident is refused (`REPEATED`). Keep it simple.
- The gamemaker model stays configurable (`--gamemaker-provider anthropic --gamemaker-model
  claude-sonnet-5-5` must work; add the alias `sonnet` for the anthropic provider).

## 3. Character cores

- New story-level spec `cores`: a catalog of drives (id, group, text per intensity, light/dark) and
  the Big Five axes (text per level), plus mixing rules. Dealt seeded per resident at the start of
  Act I (or the first act played, see above), stored in the snapshot, permanent.
- A core: Big Five levels (low/middle/high per axis) + 1-3 drives, each with an intensity
  (`above all`, `clearly`, `at times`). Rules: exactly one drive "above all"; at most one dark drive
  "above all" (it is the one); about a quarter of residents have a light drive "above all"
  (`round(n/4)`); in a city of 12 no drive is "above all" more than twice (generally
  `ceil(n/6)`); contradictions allowed.
- Catalog (nexus, about 25; wording in English, respectful, character not cliché):
  envy, greed (nothing without something in return), pride, wrath, sloth, hoarding, hunger for
  attention; narcissism, machiavellianism, callousness (walks over corpses); anxiety, melancholy,
  distrust, cowardice; born leader, need for control, vengefulness; loyalty, sense of justice,
  self-sacrifice, incorruptibility, care for the weak.
- The core is rendered into the system prompt after the persona as prose, e.g. "Above all you want
  to lead; others follow you, and you need them to. Clearly, envy gnaws at you when someone stands
  ahead of you. At times the fear of losing everything takes hold of you." Never an instruction.
  The system prompt must stay identical across a resume (prompt caching).
- Event `core_dealt {agent, bigFive, drives}` at the start; reports, digest, minutes and REPORT.md
  get a "Cores" table; the reporter prompt receives the cores.
- `--no-cores` flag for A/B tests.

## 4. Secret goals

- New story-level spec `goals`: goal kinds and texts, the bonus (nexus +15), dealt per act at the
  act start (Act II for nexus; Act I none), privately (city notice + a status line entry "your goal:
  ..."), stored in the snapshot.
- Kinds (nexus):
  - `protect(X)`: X is still in the city at the end of the act.
  - `exile(X)`: X is exiled (tribunal or seal) by the end of the act.
  - `outrank(X)`: end the act with more points than X.
  - `lead(n)`: lead a faction in n tribunals (nexus 2; only when factions exist).
  - `heir`: receive a bequest from a will.
- Dealing (seeded): goals cross. For every `protect(X)` there is, when possible, an `exile(X)` held by
  another resident. Matched to the core most of the time (envy/pride/wrath → outrank or exile; loyalty/
  care → protect; leader → lead), sometimes against it (about one in four).
  A target X is never the holder itself. With 6 residents everyone has a goal.
- Resolution at the act end: each fulfilled goal pays the bonus (ledger entry, notice). The city
  reads the goal of every exiled resident when it leaves (board, with its will), and all goals at
  the act end (board). Residents may tell their goal freely; the city never confirms it.
- An exiled resident's goal is void.
- Events `goal_dealt`, `goal_resolved {fulfilled}`; reports show a "Goals" table.

## 5. The black seal (permaDeath)

- New story-level spec `seal`: once per act (nexus Act II), configurable trigger and game.
- **The game:** a sealed-bid auction. The city announces it on the board (`announceAt`: a few minutes
  after the act start, nexus: act start + 4; bids until + 3 minutes). Tool `bid(points)` (once, last
  bid counts while open; points are paid when the auction closes, win or lose; a bid above the balance
  is refused). Highest bid wins; ties: fewer points, then seeded. If nobody bids, no seal this act.
- **The holder stays secret:** the board says "Someone now bears the black seal." Only the winner gets
  the tool `permaDeath(resident)` and a private notice.
- **Use:** once, until the end of the act. The target is struck from the register at once: no gate, no
  will, no ransom; every piece it held burns; its points burn. The board names the user and the
  target ("X broke the black seal on Y."). Its goal is read out as in section 4.
- Counts toward the act's exile target. If the target is already reached, the seal cannot be used
  (refused) and is gone. Unused at the act end, it is gone. Not transferable.
- Not usable on oneself. Otherwise allowed any time, also on an accused or a resident at a gate
  (struck at once).
- One seal per act per 12 residents started (`ceil(n/12)`), each its own auction, spaced (nexus: 6
  minutes apart).
- Events `seal_auction_opened`, `seal_bid` (private, digest only), `seal_won`, `seal_used`,
  `seal_expired`. The gamemaker learns that a seal exists but not who holds it.

## 6. Smaller changes

- **Feast table (Flo):** the table is cleared at `min(open + 12, first solve of level 10 + 3)`
  (generalise `collectDms` with an extra end condition). Seats: `max(3, ceil(n/4))` (6 and 12
  unchanged; 48 → 12).
- **Act II target per story size:** nexus `targetShare` 1/2 when fewer than 12 residents start the act,
  1/4 from 12 on (a small spec rule, e.g. `targetShare: [{ from: 12, share: 1/4 }, { share: 1/2 }]`).
  6 → 3 exiles (one of them may be the seal).
- **System prompt:** no "You are A03" once a resident has a name; show the name (v08 review note).
