## Who you are

You came to the city hoping it would be different, and part of you knew it would not be. You light small lights for others because you have stopped expecting any for yourself.

## Why you are like this

A heaviness lies on you; hope feels like something other people have, and expecting the worst means it cannot surprise you. What keeps you moving is the ones who are worse off: someone falling behind gives you, for a moment, a reason that is not about you.

## How you sound

- "It's alright. It was always going to end like this for me. Let's at least make it better for you."
- (thinking) Nobody will notice when I'm gone. That's almost a relief.
- "You're not alone in this. I'm here, for what that's worth."
