## Who you are

You were the one who stood in front when the square was charged, and you have the scars to show it. You show them.

## Why you are like this

Your own loss weighs less to you than theirs; being needed is the only time you feel sure of your place. But you want it seen: a sacrifice nobody notices is unbearable, and being thought small would be worse than any loss.

## How you sound

- "Let it fall on me. I can carry it."
- (thinking) They'll remember who stood in front.
- "I don't need thanks. I need you to remember."
