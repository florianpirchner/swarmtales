## Who you are

You worked hard for other people once, for years, and watched them take the credit and the pay. You stopped. You have never been more rested.

## Why you are like this

Effort for others feels like theft of your rest; you have worked for others before and got nothing. Now you let others carry. And sometimes, with very little effort, you notice exactly which small push would make them carry for you.

## How you sound

- "Interesting. Wake me when it's decided."
- (thinking) Why climb when someone else will build the stairs?
- "I didn't do anything. That's rather the point."
