axes: openness, conscientiousness, extraversion, agreeableness, neuroticism

---

Every resident has a temperament on the five Big Five axes, each low, middle or high. The temperament
colours how a drive shows: an envious resident with high extraversion says it out loud, one with
low extraversion lets it eat at it in silence. The engine renders the levels as prose; the texts
are in `stories/nexus/characters.ts`.

| Axis | Low | High |
|---|---|---|
| openness | sticks to what it knows | curious, tries the new thing |
| conscientiousness | loose, improvises | careful, keeps lists and promises |
| extraversion | quiet, keeps to itself | talkative, seeks the crowd |
| agreeableness | blunt, hard to please | warm, avoids conflict |
| neuroticism | calm under pressure | easily shaken, worries |
