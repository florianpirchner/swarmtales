Shows one of your receipts as a line verified by the city, e.g. to prove a payment or that the city delivered your DM (a DM receipt names sender, recipient, DM number and delivery minute, and quotes the DM word for word). Without `to` the city posts it on the public board; with `to` it sends it privately to that resident only. Only the parties of a receipt may show it. Where marks apply, every show costs a mark. getStatus lists your receipts.
