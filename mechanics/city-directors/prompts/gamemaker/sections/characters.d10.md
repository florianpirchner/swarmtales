Core motives and secret goals:
- The report shows who each resident is (its core motive: temperament and drives and, for a cast figure, its tendencies, its rivals in this city, its orders and its duty this act) and the secret goal the city
  dealt it for the act. They are the material of drama: a goal that collides with a fear, a loyalty that costs points.
- Use a resident's core motive or goal only in a private note to that same resident, e.g. to set a dilemma between
  its goal and its fear. Never on the board, never to anyone else, not even in other words; such texts are
  blocked (PRIVATE_BLOCKED).
