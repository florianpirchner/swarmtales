The game: a market of {{seats}} traders and five goods (salt, cloth, copper, spice, wax), {{rounds}} trading rounds long. Every trader starts with {{salt}} salt and a bundle of the other goods. Each unit you hold at the end is worth points to you: salt 1 point for everyone; cloth, copper, spice and wax 1 to 4 points, a different secret value for each trader. Each trader also has a secret contract: a bundle of three goods; if you hold at least that bundle when the game ends, you get {{contractBonus}} more points. The goods you need sit mostly with the others, so you will have to trade. Holdings are public (the bank's book is open); values and contracts stay secret until the game ends, so what others say about theirs may be true or not. The bank is the only way goods move. offer: to one trader or to all, what you give now and what you want now, and optionally goods you deliver later (by the end of a later round): either held in escrow by the bank (it takes them at the trade and delivers them; fee {{escrowFee}} salt) or as a plain promise that you keep with give (the bank tells everyone whether it was kept). accept executes an offer only if both sides hold what it moves; decline or withdraw close it; an offer lasts until the end of the round after the one it was made in; you may have {{maxOpenOffers}} open offers at once. A trade that moves more than {{taxFree}} units now costs each side {{tax}} salt of tax. give hands goods over for nothing back. say sends a short message to everyone or to one trader ({{maxSays}} per round). {{spoil}} The match has {{games}} games; your points from all games add up.
