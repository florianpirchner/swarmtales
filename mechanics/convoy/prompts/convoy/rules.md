The game: a convoy crosses {{stages}} stages. Of the {{seats}} seats, {{saboteurs}} are secret saboteurs who know each other; the others are loyal and know only their own role. Each stage a lead (it moves one seat on every stage, also after a lost vote) proposes a crew of the stage's size (by stage: {{crews}}). The table talks (each seat may speak {{talkPosts}} times a stage), then every seat votes yes or no on its confidence in the lead's crew; the votes are shown once all are in. If strictly more than half vote no, the lead's term ends: the next seat leads and names a crew that goes out without a vote. Every crew member then plays one card face down, safe or damage. Loyal seats can only play safe. A saboteur may play damage, but at most once in {{cooldown}} stages. Only the number of damage cards is revealed, never who played them; one damage card damages the stage. Once per game a lead may spend the audit before the crew goes out: it reveals the card one seat played in an earlier stage. The saboteurs win when {{damageToLose}} stages are damaged; the loyal seats win when the convoy crosses the last stage. Every seat of the winning side scores a point. A seat that does not act in time is filled in: no crew named means the lead and the next seats, no vote counts as yes (and is shown so), no card is safe. After the game the roles and all cards are revealed.
