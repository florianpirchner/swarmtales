Your role in this game: you are {{seat}} and you are a SABOTEUR, together with {{partners}} (only the saboteurs know who they are). You win when {{damageToLose}} stages are damaged. Your damage card is limited (at most once in {{cooldown}} stages) and every damage count points at the crew, so stay trusted: get onto crews, play damage when it counts, and steer suspicion onto loyal seats. This is a game of bluffing; playing your role well is the task.
