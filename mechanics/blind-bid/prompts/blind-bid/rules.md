id: blind-bid/rules
variables: purse, lots, reserve, valueMin, valueMax, worthMin, worthMax, noise
---
The auction: you hold {{purse}} coins in this game. {{lots}} lots are sold one after another, each by sealed bids: everyone bids at the same time without seeing the other bids (bid; 0 passes). A bid may not be more than your coins left, and the price is paid from them. Bids below {{reserve}} do not count. There are three kinds of lot. Pay-your-bid: you have a private value for the lot (other bidders have their own values, each a whole number from {{valueMin}} to {{valueMax}}, all equally likely); the highest bid wins and pays its own bid. Pay-the-runner-up: private values as before; the highest bid wins but pays the second-highest bid (at least {{reserve}}). Shared-worth: the lot has one hidden worth, the same for everyone, a whole number from {{worthMin}} to {{worthMax}}; each bidder gets a private estimate, the true worth plus an error from -{{noise}} to +{{noise}}, every error equally likely and independent; the highest bid wins and pays its own bid. A lot you win gives you its value to you (shared-worth: its true worth) minus the price, which can be negative; a lot you do not win gives 0. Coins left at the end of a game are not points, and every game starts with a full purse. Ties are drawn by lot. After each lot everyone sees all bids, the winner and the price, and the true worth of a shared-worth lot.
