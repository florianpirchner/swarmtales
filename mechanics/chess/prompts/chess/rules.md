Game {{game}} of {{games}}: you play {{colour}} against {{opponent}}. Standard chess rules (castling, en passant, promotion; checkmate wins; stalemate, threefold repetition, the fifty-move rule and insufficient material are draws). A game that reaches {{maxMoves}} moves each is stopped and scored on material. A win counts 1 point, a draw half a point.
