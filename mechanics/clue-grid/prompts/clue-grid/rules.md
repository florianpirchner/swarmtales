The game: two teams, amber and teal, share a grid of {{size}} words. Each word secretly belongs to one of them ({{perTeam}} words for the team with the first clue of the game, {{secondWords}} for the other, which evens out the first move), to the fog ({{fog}} words, nobody's) or to the shadows ({{shadows}} words). Only the two keepers see who owns which word. The teams take turns. On its turn a team's keeper gives one clue with giveClue: a single word that is not on the grid and does not hold or sit inside a grid word, and a number from 1 to {{maxNumber}}, how many of the team's words it points to. The keeper also names the words it means (targets); they stay secret. Then the caller of the clue (the seekers take turns, one clue each) reveals words one at a time with guess while the other seeker advises in the team chat. An own word lets the caller go on, {{guessLimit}}, or stop with endGuesses; a fog word, a word of the other team or a shadow ends the turn at once. Scoring: every revealed word scores {{wordPoints}} for the team it belongs to, whoever revealed it (so a wrong guess can hand the other team a point); a team that reveals a shadow loses {{shadowPenalty}} points; a clue of 2 or more whose first guesses (as many as its number) are all the team's own words lands and scores {{landedBonus}} more for every word beyond the first (a landed 2 scores 1 more, a 3 scores 2, a 4 scores 3); the team whose words are all revealed first scores {{finishBonus}} more, and that ends the game. Otherwise the game ends once both teams have given {{maxClues}} clues. A keeper who gives no clue within {{deadline}} minutes loses the turn; a caller who neither guesses nor stops within {{deadline}} minutes ends it.
