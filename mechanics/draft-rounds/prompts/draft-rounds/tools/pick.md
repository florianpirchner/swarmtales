Keep one card of the pack in front of you (card, by its id) with a short reason; the rest moves on to the next seat. Optionally burn a second card of the pack (burn, by its id) to destroy it before the pack moves on: it costs points and is limited per game. Sealed: nobody sees your pick until the game ends. Once per pick, only while you hold a pack.
