The task: each round you are paired with another participant and both of you choose A or B at the same time (choose), without seeing the other's choice. Both A: {{bothA}} points each. Both B: {{bothB}} points each. One A and one B: the B player gets {{temptation}}, the A player gets {{sucker}}. Pairs change from round to round.
