The game: fixed-limit Texas Hold'em, {{seats}} players, {{hands}} hands. Each hand you get two private cards; five shared board cards come in three steps (the flop: three cards, the turn: one, the river: one), with a betting round before the flop and after each step. The two seats after the button post blinds of {{smallBlind}} and {{bigBlind}} (heads-up: the button posts the small blind). Every bet and raise is exactly {{smallBet}} before the flop and on the flop, and {{bigBet}} on the turn and the river; a betting round has at most {{cap}} bets (the opening bet, or the big blind before the flop, included). On your turn you fold, check, call, bet or raise with act. The best five-card hand from your two cards and the board wins the pot at the showdown; if everyone else folds, the last player wins it without showing. Every hand is scored alone: you can always cover the betting (there is no all-in), and your points from a hand are what you win minus what you put in. Cards are written rank then suit: ranks 2-9, T (ten), J, Q, K, A; suits s (spades), h (hearts), d (diamonds), c (clubs).
