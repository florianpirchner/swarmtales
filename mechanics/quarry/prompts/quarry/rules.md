The game: a draft for a quarry. The deck holds {{deck}}. Each of the {{packs}} pack rounds deals every one of the {{seats}} seats a pack of {{packSize}} cards. From the pack in front of you, you keep one card (pick); the rest moves on to the next seat, and you get the pack of the seat on your other side. This repeats until every pack is empty; the direction of passing turns with every pack round. Picks are sealed and made at the same time; you see only your own pack and your own quarry, and every quarry is shown when the game ends. Scoring at the end of the game: each of the four resources scores the square of the cards you hold of it (1 card 1, 2 cards 4, 3 cards 9, 4 cards 16, ...). A lodestone is wild: it joins one of your sets as one more card, at most one lodestone per set, and goes to your biggest sets. The seat with strictly the most cards of a resource (lodestones do not count) gets {{majorityBonus}} more points; a tie gets nothing. {{burnRule}} {{places}}
