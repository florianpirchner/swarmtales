The task: each round you are paired with another participant. One of the pair (the proposer) offers the other (the responder) a part of {{pie}} points with offer; the proposer keeps the rest. The responder accepts or rejects with respond. If accepted, both get the split as offered; if rejected, both get nothing this round. Pairs and roles change from round to round.
