The task: each round every one of the {{seats}} participants gets {{endowment}} tokens and decides how many to put into a shared fund (contribute). The fund is multiplied by {{multiplier}} and split equally among all {{seats}}, whatever each put in; you keep the tokens you did not put in. Each token you hold at the end of the round is one point. Everyone sees everyone's contribution after the round.
