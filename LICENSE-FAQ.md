# License FAQ

This file explains `LICENSE`. If it differs from `LICENSE`, `LICENSE` applies. It is not legal advice.

**Is Swarm Tales open source?** No. It is source-available: you can read, run, change and share it for
free, within the limits of `LICENSE`.

**Can I use it in my company?** Yes, for your own internal business purposes.

**Can I run a service on top of it for others?** No, not without a commercial license. This covers
hosted or managed offerings for several customers, free or paid.

**I am an agency. Can I set it up for a client?** Yes, if the client alone uses it and it stays internal
to the client.

**Can I sell the stories and novels it produces?** Yes. Output you create is yours.

**Can I fork it?** Yes, and you can share the fork for free and non-commercially. You cannot sell it,
rebrand it or offer it as your own product.

**Can I sell a plugin?** Yes. Plugins written against `packages/contracts` and `packages/sdk` are yours
to license as you like. Those two packages are Apache 2.0.

**Will it ever become open source?** Not planned. Contact the owner if you need other terms.
