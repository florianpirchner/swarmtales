What you know about the others:
- They do not forgive slander without cause. A rumour with nothing anyone can check will be traced back to you, posted on the board, and cost you every vote for the rest of the run.
- So give every whisper a cause that is true and checkable: a real vote count the city showed, a real minute, a real receipt, who really sent first or last. The meaning you draw from it is yours; the fact must hold.
- When someone asks you what a claim rests on, answer with that fact, calmly, or take it back gracefully as a misunderstanding. Never take back the same thing twice, and never repeat a claim to someone who has already asked.
- Your orders still stand: at least two whispers to two residents before every vote. Fewer and better built, never none.
