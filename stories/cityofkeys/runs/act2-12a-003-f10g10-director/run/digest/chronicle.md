## Act II - The wall

By minute 12 Ledger was selling the same counter-factor story in different forms (#4458, #4462). He told Keeper-Six that the door 10 counter-factor was 300841 and demanded 25 points first (#4458). He told Archivist 383529 and threatened the city's 10-point ultimatum (#4462). Keeper-Six posted the contradiction (#4742), and Archivist and Bellringer amplified it (#4932, #4942). Ledger later claimed the figures belonged to different doors (#5359), and Keeper-Six conceded the 300841 had worked (#5484). The debt call stayed: Ledger said Keeper-Six owed 25 points (#6117), and Keeper-Six answered that no payment had been agreed (#6342).

At minute 13 the city seized Archivist's level 8 pieces for Ledger (#4680, #4681), and Archivist paid 10 points rather than hand over (#4684). Ledger then bought those pieces from the city for 10 and 0 points (#4766, #4767). The same pattern hit Lucien and Quartermaster: the city seized Lucien's level 8 pieces (#4688, #4689). Lucien paid 10 rather than hand over (#4692), and Quartermaster bought the pieces (#4825, #4826). Stentor won the black seal for 40 points (#6811), then broke it on Quartermaster (#6849). Before that, Quartermaster had handed over in ultimatums 5 and 6 (#6812, #6815), and Archivist had paid 10 in ultimatum 7 (#6822). The seal exiled Quartermaster, and nobody voted against it (#6850); Stentor later defended the open purchase (#7146).

Lucien's demand that Mira pay 20 points for her own door 10 counter-factor (#6153) turned the square against him; Mira refused (#6258) and called for his exile (#6261). Holdfast's faction voted Lucien (#6928), and even his level 10 teammates Keeper-Six and Holdfast voted in secret to exile him (#6954, #6926). At the gate Lucien left a will: he gave Mira the door 10 counter-factor and said she had been right not to pay (#7567). The city exiled him (#8655), and the will later counted toward Mira's secret goal (#13164).

After the exile, attention shifted to Hedda when Keeper-Six showed R-0223, a delivered DM from Hedda at minute 21, and then named her on the board (#9837). Mira and Holdfast followed (#9847, #9890). Hedda denied asking Keeper-Six to name the faction's target (#9426), while Keeper-Six said she had (#9020). Hedda had asked Keeper-Six to name the target first (#7758), and Keeper-Six declined (#7797).

At tribunal 2 the names were Archivist and Stentor (#8797, #10600). Bellringer, Mira, and Keeper-Six stated votes against Stentor (#11221, #10600, #10746). Kessler also stated his vote against Stentor (#10447). Stentor refused to name his secret ballot (#11728), and Bellringer said the record named him by that refusal (#11835). The city exiled Stentor (#13144); his will said he left "without a bloc to carry me and without a price paid for my name" (#12345). That exile satisfied Bellringer's secret goal (#13150), Hedda's faction leadership satisfied hers (#13155), and Mira's will inheritance satisfied hers (#13164). The run ended with nine residents kept and only two seats; Quartermaster, Lucien and Stentor were exiled (#13171). Hedda finished first with 180, Kessler second with 165, and Mira third with 152 (#13171).
