# Pack 01: The Wall and the Clock

Point of view: the Gamemaker, minutes 0-12.

## The plan (outline)

- He opens the square with the rank spread and the warning that a kept shard pays nobody, then watches the first alliances form over the low doors.
- A whisper that would have exposed Kessler's private thinking is stopped by the city, and the Gamemaker learns the guard will not let him spend a resident's mind as theatre.
- He posts the second rank line, presses Mira, Wren, Keeper-Six, Hedda, Lucien and Holdfast with private notes, and tries to put a price on every stalled piece.
- As the wall fills, ten residents wait on three level-seven doors; he names the waiters in the square and pushes the holders with the cheap first miss.
- He sets two clocked demands, Archivist against Ledger and Lucien against Quartermaster, to turn old standoffs into visible choices.
- When the first refusals arrive, Lucien and Archivist both refuse to give first, and the Gamemaker sees his private pressure become public defiance.
- He posts the rank spread again and claims every standoff now has a live pressure; the hook is the first deadline, where refusal itself will cost marks.

## The Gamemaker and his team

- The Gamemaker: the city's maker of games. He thinks aloud, reads the residents, sends notes, sets ultimatums, calls tribunals and posts to the board. His recorded thoughts are his inner voice.
- The Courier: carries his notes to the residents (they sign as COURIER) and brings back their refusals.
- The Guard: stops a note that would betray what a resident privately thinks; the city records each note it stopped.
- The Scribe: reads every message and thought of the residents and names what it shows (hesitation, a threat, a plea, a lie); the Gamemaker reads its reads.
