# Pack 06: The Last Receipt

Point of view: the Gamemaker, minutes 44-49.

## The plan (outline)

- He posts the last line in the square: the gate is about to shut, Stentor's hundred and four marks are unclaimed, Hedda stands first and has answered the receipt without a yes or a no, and Archivist still has an unread delivery before door eight.
- He sends his last private beats, urging Hedda to correct the record and Mira and Wren to bid for Stentor's heir line, but the refusals come back: no one will buy an inheritance from a man at the gate.
- When the gate shuts, the city puts Stentor beyond the wall; eight ballots are recorded, no heir is named, and his marks die with him.
- The city records that Bellringer's secret goal is kept by Stentor's exile, that Hedda has led a bloc in two counts, and that Mira's last word from Lucien has counted.
- The run ends with nine residents kept and only two seats: Hedda first with one hundred eighty, Kessler second with one hundred sixty-five, Mira third with one hundred fifty-two, Keeper-Six fourth with one hundred fifty, Wren fifth with one hundred fifty, Bellringer sixth with one hundred forty-six, Holdfast seventh with one hundred forty, Ledger eighth with one hundred twenty, Archivist ninth with sixty, Stentor tenth with zero, Quartermaster eleventh with zero, Lucien twelfth with zero.
- The Gamemaker has his verdict and his ranking, but the quiet hand he never scripted stands at the top; the city closes on the two who kept their names and their receipts.

## The Gamemaker and his team

- The Gamemaker: the city's maker of games. He thinks aloud, reads the residents, sends notes, sets ultimatums, calls tribunals and posts to the board. His recorded thoughts are his inner voice.
- The Courier: carries his notes to the residents (they sign as COURIER) and brings back their refusals.
- The Guard: stops a note that would betray what a resident privately thinks; the city records each note it stopped.
- The Scribe: reads every message and thought of the residents and names what it shows (hesitation, a threat, a plea, a lie); the Gamemaker reads its reads.
