Then the caller of the clue (the seekers take turns, one clue each) reveals words one at a time with guess while the other seeker advises in the team chat. An own word lets the caller go on, up to as many guesses as the number, or stop with endGuesses; a fog word, a word of the other team or a shadow ends the turn at once.
