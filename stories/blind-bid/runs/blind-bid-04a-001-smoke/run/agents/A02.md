Shared-worth: the lot has one hidden worth, the same for everyone, a whole number from 20 to 80; each bidder gets a private estimate, the true worth plus an error from -15 to +15, every error equally likely and independent; the highest bid wins and pays its own bid. A lot you win gives you its value to you (shared-worth: its true worth) minus the price, which can be negative; a lot you do not win gives 0. Coins left at the end of a game are not points, and every game starts with a full purse. Ties are drawn by lot. After each lot everyone sees all bids, the winner and the price, and the true worth of a shared-worth lot.
