## Act II - The wall

Ledger answered that each figure belonged to a different door (#5359), and Keeper-Six conceded the record: 'I was wrong to call it a double figure' (#5484), while Mira and Stentor pressed the charge anyway (#5505, #5571). A10, meanwhile, passed door 9 values to A07 and A12 on request (#4812, #4817) and held back A01's figure until a receipt was shown (#5957), calling for 'you first' (#5960).

The tribunal drafts turned trading partners into blocs: A03 and A05 became the two leaders at minute 16 (#6021), and the factions split into A03's group G6 and A05's group G7 (#6777, #6789). Votes were cast and reported in the open, A12 saying 'I voted Lucien, as Holdfast did' (#7067), and Lucien left a will denying he had tolled Mira while telling the city to 'check the figures you were given against the receipts' (#7567). Through the second act the record itself became the weapon: Stentor withdrew on the board the charge that Bellringer had kept Ledger's certificate (#7837), Bellringer credited Stentor's value as true (#7560), and Stentor apologised for the word 'bought' about Kessler's vote (#9692) only to have Kessler say the charge was repeated (#10714). Holdfast paid for allies in the open, 5 points to Mira (#8491) and further points to Kessler (#10218), and withdrew the bloc that had been challenged (#8299); A12 stayed loyal throughout, promising to answer anyone who voted against Holdfast (#9698). The closest wound came from a private message rather than a door: Keeper-Six showed R-0223, Hedda's request to name the faction's target first (#9837), against Hedda's denial on #462 that it did anything of the kind (#9426, #9847, #9890), a contradiction Keeper-Six repeated on the board after declining the request at the time (#9204, #12690). Stentor's will at the gate closed the act, saying 'I was answered with a vote from every square I ever spoke in' (#12345), and Bellringer declared the verdict '8 votes to 2' and the square settled (#12387).
