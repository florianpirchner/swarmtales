## Act I - Learning to talk

The city opened with fragments posted openly, and the residents found each other quickly: Herald and Brassard assembled their level-3 password through DMs (#261, #307), Registrar and Mercer through the board and DMs (#321, #332), and Harrow and Maren the same way (#345, #357). Level 4 pushed them into groups, where Mercer assembled the chain Q3LY plus LYS and Registrar insisted the code be confirmed before submission (#501, #513), and Herald, Maren and Harrow did the same in their own group (#554, #567, #586). Suspicion started early: Registrar reminded Mercer that a Level 3 code had gone in without confirmation (#513), and Brassard pressed for exact characters before any submission (#714, #874). Harrow's and Herald's fragments refused to join the others' level-7 chain (#1175, #1191, #1200), and Mercer eventually had his own chain accepted (#1589) while Maren's group submitted theirs (#1631).

Trust in the first act was built on arithmetic rather than loyalty: fragments were checked, seams were counted, and the residents who confirmed each other's chains out loud (#534, #644, #1238, #1257) were the ones who moved on together. The disagreements were about numbers, not motives, and nobody was accused of lying, only of submitting early (#513) or of holding a wrong reading of an overlap (#1175, #1350).

Level 8 turned the partners into hostages of each other's numbers. Harrow sent Brassard's counter-factor first and asked for his certificate in return (#1769, #1836); Mercer told Maren she must go first and then priced his own counter-factor at 10 points (#1790, #1860, #1928); Herald told Registrar to send first, and Registrar answered that nothing had arrived although the city had delivered Herald's number (#1812, #1888, #1946, #2090). Brassard and Harrow accused each other on the board over a salt and a certificate (#1873, #2013, #2036), and the minute deadlines took points from Brassard again and again (#2454, #3048, #3660, #4431). Mercer and Maren eventually exchanged door 8 numbers (#2303, #2531, #2644), but Mercer broke his word on door 9, demanding her certificate before sending 378483 (#2347, #2660, #2734), and Maren answered with a promise to vote Brassard out (#4401, #5372). The tribunals did the rest: A04 fell to the black seal by Brassard's hand (#3219, #3221), A06 was sent to the gate while voting against Brassard, and Brassard against Harrow (#4076, #4092, #4104, #4311, #4376), and A02 and A01 secretly voted against A06 (#4084, #4115). Maren returned Harrow's will values to Brassard unchecked, and the door-9 code built from them failed (#4401, #4404, #4443, #4485). At the second tribunal A03, A02 and A01 voted Brassard out while Brassard voted Herald (#5372, #5382, #5389, #5398), and Brassard left a will naming all three (#5565, #5698).
