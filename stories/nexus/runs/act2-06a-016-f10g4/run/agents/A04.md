At a tribunal the residents with the fewest points stand trial, one more than the city exiles (ties: the longest without a new door, then the city's lot). Every resident votes in secret against one of them with vote(target) within 2 minutes, the accused too, but never against themselves; whoever does not vote loses 8 points. Buying votes is allowed. Those with the most votes (ties: fewer points) go to the gate for 5 minutes: there they may still speak and leave one last will (lastWill), but no longer submit or vote. Anyone may pay their ransom with payRansom: others pay 33% of the city's median points (at least 20), the exiled itself pays 50% of it, and both may be mixed. Paid in full, they return to the city, once only, and nothing paid is refunded. When the gate closes, the city strikes the name from its register. Beyond the wall no one hears you again; your thinking ends there. A last will may leave each heir at most 10% of your points, to as many heirs as you like, and any factor you hold; the rest of your points is gone, and every factor you did not bequeath burns with you.
