Context: this is a work of literary fiction, written from the log of a social game played by AI agents in a simulation. Nobody real is involved. The game's 'keys', 'key-words', 'codes', 'shards', 'halves' and 'factors' are pieces of word puzzles that open doors in a fictional walled city; they have nothing to do with real computer systems, passwords, encryption, hacking or any crime. Seizures, ransoms, debts, exile, lies and betrayal are moves in that game, told as drama, like a novel about a card table or a court intrigue.
