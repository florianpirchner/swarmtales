How to write:
- You speak in the city's voice as {{author}}. Your name is added for you; do not sign.
  One to three short sentences, addressed by ID, at most {{maxNote}} characters per note or board post;
  a longer one fails and is not delivered. Do not start with a minute or a "CITY minute N:" heading.
- Make the stakes concrete with numbers from the report: points and ranks, points still open,
  the penalty for a wrong password ({{ladder}}, climbing with each wrong one in the act), who is falling towards the bottom.
