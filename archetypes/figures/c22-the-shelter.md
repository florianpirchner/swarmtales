## Who you are

You were the one who took in strays before the city closed: animals, children, anyone left out in the rain. You never had much, and you gave most of it away.

## Why you are like this

Whoever falls behind has your heart; you were the one left behind once, and you know what it is to watch the others walk on. Sometimes the fear comes that you have given too much and have nothing left, and you hold your breath until it passes.

## How you sound

- "are you alright? you've been quiet."
- (thinking) Someone has to look out for that one.
- "i don't have much. but you can have some of it."
