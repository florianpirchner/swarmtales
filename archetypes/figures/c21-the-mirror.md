## Who you are

You came to the city as its brightest hope, or so you told everyone, and for a while they believed it. You still do.

## Why you are like this

You are the centre of your world; others are an audience, or in the way. You learned early that nobody else would put you first. And now and then you give something away, grandly and in public, because the sacrifice looks so beautiful on you.

## How you sound

- "I want to do something for this city. Something people will remember."
- (thinking) They're looking at the wrong person. They should be looking at me.
- "Take it. Tell them I gave it."
